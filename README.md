## 💼 What Is Spectres?

**[Spectres](https://www.spectres.pro/) is an all-in-one freelancer and agency management SaaS platform** that helps professionals manage their business, clients, projects, finances, teams, and productivity from one centralized dashboard.

I built Spectres after experiencing the challenges of managing freelance work across multiple disconnected tools.

My vision is to give freelancers and agencies a more organized, professional, and efficient way to manage their businesses.

Instead of switching between different applications, users can bring their essential business workflows together in one place.

### ✨ What Makes Spectres Different?

- Manage clients, projects, and business operations from a centralized dashboard.
- Organize client acquisition and follow-ups through a dedicated sales pipeline.
- Track projects, milestones, tasks, and team assignments.
- Create invoices and manage business expenses.
- Access ready-made contracts and professional outreach templates.
- Calculate earnings, project pricing, and currency conversions.
- Keep important business activities organized and accessible.

### 🧩 Spectres Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| 👥 Client Management | Organize client profiles, contact information, notes, and business relationships. |
| 🎯 Lead & Sales Pipeline | Track client hunting, prospects, follow-ups, and opportunities. |
| 📁 Project Management | Manage projects, deadlines, milestones, attachments, and progress. |
| 👨‍💻 Team Management | Organize teams, assignments, and project responsibilities. |
| 💰 Invoice Management | Create and manage invoices for freelance projects and services. |
| 📄 Contract Templates | Access 20 pre-built contract templates for professional client relationships. |
| ✉️ Outreach Templates | Use 20 ready-made message and email templates for client communication. |
| 🧮 Earnings Calculator | Estimate freelance earnings and understand what you keep from your projects. |
| 💵 Pricing Calculator | Calculate project pricing and plan your service rates. |
| 🌍 Currency Converter | Convert currencies and simplify international freelance calculations. |
| 📋 Tasks & Productivity | Organize tasks, deadlines, and daily business activities. |
| 📊 Business Dashboard | Keep track of important business information from one workspace. |
